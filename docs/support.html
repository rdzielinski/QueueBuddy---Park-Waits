Frequently asked

The wait times look wrong or out of date

Wait times come from third-party data sources (themeparks.wiki and queue-times.com) and are estimates. They can lag the parks by a few minutes or occasionally be unavailable. Pull down to refresh, and always treat the numbers as a guide rather than a guarantee.

I'm not getting wait-time notifications

Three things to check: (1) allow notifications for QueueBuddy in iOS Settings → Notifications; (2) star the attractions you want to watch; and (3) set a wait threshold for each favorite. Notifications fire when a favorited ride drops below the threshold you chose. Background updates also depend on iOS Background App Refresh being enabled.

What does the ● SOLO badge mean?

It marks a single-rider lane — a separate, usually faster queue where the ride fills in lone seats. You may not sit with your group, but it's often the quickest way onto a headliner. The badge makes single-rider entries easy to spot next to the standby version.

How do I use the AI day planner?

The planner uses an AI provider of your choice (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, or Google Gemini) and requires your own API key, entered in Settings. Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain and is sent only to that provider, directly from your device — never to us. Any usage is billed by the provider against your key.

My widget or Live Activity isn't updating

The home-screen widget reads the latest data the app has fetched, so open QueueBuddy once after installing (and occasionally during your day) to refresh the shared cache. Make sure the app has Background App Refresh enabled so it can update while backgrounded.

Why does QueueBuddy ask for my location?

Location is optional and "while-in-use" only. It's used on-device to detect when you arrive at a park and surface nearby waits. Your location is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. You can decline it and still use the app.

Which parks are supported?

All four Walt Disney World parks (Magic Kingdom, EPCOT, Hollywood Studios, Animal Kingdom) and all three Universal Orlando parks (Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure, and Epic Universe).
